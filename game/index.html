SOLARA BAY

An original open-world crime game

You are Raka, a driver new to Solara Bay. Steal cars, outrun the police, run jobs for Mara, and survive the night the crew turns on you. Explore the city on foot or by car — traffic, pedestrians, the wanted level and six missions are all live.

Controls: WASD to move / drive, mouse drag to look, E to get in and out of a car, F to shoot, Space to jump, M for missions, P to pause.
