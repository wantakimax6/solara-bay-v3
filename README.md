Solara Bay is an original smooth blocky low-poly 3D open-world crime game in third person: a sun-soaked bay city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and stickman pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who chase you, six missions with a story — including a hunt after an informant leaks your position — combat with natural ragdoll falls, a HUD with minimap, a start screen, and saving. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move and drive, mouse drag to look, E to enter and exit cars, F to shoot, Space to jump, M for missions, P to pause.
